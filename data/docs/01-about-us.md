# About Bean & Barrel Coffee Co.

## Who we are
Bean & Barrel Coffee Co. is an online shop based in Manchester, UK, selling home espresso machines, grinders and brewing accessories. We were founded in 2019 by two former baristas and ship to the UK and the European Union.

## Showroom
Our Manchester showroom is open Thursday to Saturday, 10:00 to 16:00. You can try any machine on display, and our staff can help you dial in a grinder. Appointments are not needed, but you can book a free 30-minute one-to-one demo through the "Visit us" page.
