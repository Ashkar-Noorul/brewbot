# Warranty

## What is covered
- Espresso machines: 2-year warranty from the delivery date, extended to 3 years if you register the machine within 60 days of delivery.
- Grinders: 2-year warranty on the motor and electronics. Burrs are wear parts and are covered for 6 months against manufacturing defects only.
- Accessories (tampers, jugs, scales): 1 year.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and component failures in normal home use.

## What is not covered
- Damage caused by limescale. Machines must be descaled as described in the care guide; we may ask for evidence such as a descaler purchase.
- Use in a commercial setting such as a café or office with more than 10 people.
- Accidental damage, drops, or damage from using the wrong voltage.
- Repairs or modifications by anyone other than Bean & Barrel or an authorised repair partner.

## Registering your machine
Register on the "Register my machine" page with your order number and the serial number printed on the label under the drip tray.

## Making a warranty claim
Email support with your order number, the serial number, a description of the problem and, if possible, a short video. We will try remote troubleshooting first. If the machine needs repair we send a prepaid courier box; repairs usually take 10 working days from when we receive the machine. If it can't be repaired, we replace it with the same or an equivalent model.
