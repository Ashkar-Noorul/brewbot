# Shipping Policy

## UK delivery
- Standard delivery (2 to 4 working days): £4.95, free on orders over £60.
- Next-day delivery: £9.95 for orders placed before 14:00 Monday to Thursday. Orders placed after 14:00 on Thursday, or on Friday to Sunday, arrive on Tuesday.
- Large items (espresso machines over 15 kg) are delivered by a two-person courier, and you will be asked to choose a delivery slot by text message.

## EU delivery
EU delivery costs £14.95 and usually takes 4 to 7 working days. Orders are shipped Delivered Duty Paid (DDP), so there are no import charges or customs fees to pay on arrival. We do not currently ship to Switzerland, Norway or outside Europe.

## Dispatch times
Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are dispatched the same day. Pre-order items show their expected dispatch date on the product page and in your order confirmation email.

## Tracking your order
You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order is dispatched. You can also check your order status by giving BrewBot your order number, which starts with "BB-" followed by six digits (for example BB-104233).

## Lost or damaged parcels
If your tracking has not updated for 5 working days, or your parcel arrives damaged, contact support within 14 days of the expected delivery date. Please keep all packaging and take photos of any damage. We will send a replacement or a full refund once the courier investigation is opened; you do not have to wait for it to finish.
