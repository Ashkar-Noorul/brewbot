# Barrel Club Coffee Subscription

## How it works
Barrel Club sends freshly roasted beans from independent UK roasters every 2 or 4 weeks. Each delivery is 2 bags of 250 g. Beans are roasted no more than 5 days before dispatch.

## Price
£16 per delivery, including UK postage. The subscription is not available for EU delivery.

## Choosing your coffee
Pick a profile when you sign up: "Classic" (chocolatey, nutty, good for milk drinks), "Bright" (fruity, light roasts for filter) or "Roaster's Choice" (a rotating mix). You can change your profile at any time before the next billing date.

## Pausing, skipping and cancelling
Skip a delivery, pause for up to 3 months, or cancel at any time from the "My subscription" page. Changes must be made at least 2 days before the next billing date to affect that delivery. There is no minimum term and no cancellation fee.

## Member discount
Barrel Club members get 10% off espresso machines, grinders and accessories (not other coffee) while their subscription is active. The discount is applied automatically when you are signed in.
