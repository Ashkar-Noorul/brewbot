# Nimbus 64 Flat Burr Grinder

## Overview
The Nimbus 64 is a single-dose flat burr grinder priced at £449. It uses 64 mm steel flat burrs and has stepless grind adjustment, so it works for espresso, filter and French press. Retention is under 0.2 g.

## Grind settings
The dial is numbered 0 to 10, with fine adjustment between the numbers.
- Espresso: start at 2.5 and adjust in small steps until your shot runs in 25 to 32 seconds.
- Pour-over and filter: around 6.
- French press: around 8.5.

Always adjust the grind setting with the motor running or the chamber empty, never with beans sitting between the burrs.

## Cleaning
Brush out the chute weekly. Every month, remove the top burr by turning the burr carrier anticlockwise and brush both burrs. Do not wash burrs with water. You can use grinder cleaning pellets (not rice) every two months.

## Burr life and replacement
The burrs last roughly 500 kg of coffee for typical home use. Replacement burr sets cost £79 and can be fitted at home in about 10 minutes.
