# Returns and Refunds

## 30-day returns
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they are unused and in their original packaging. Start a return from the "My orders" page or by emailing support with your order number. Returns are free for UK customers using the prepaid label we email you. EU customers pay their own return postage.

## Opened or used items
Espresso machines and grinders that have been used can still be returned within 30 days, but a 15% restocking fee applies to cover cleaning and inspection. The item must be complete with all accessories. Grinders must be returned empty of beans; we cannot accept grinders containing coffee for hygiene reasons.

The following cannot be returned once opened: coffee beans, cleaning tablets and descaler, milk jugs that have been used, and gift cards.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 working days of the return arriving at our warehouse. You will get an email when the refund is processed. Your bank may take a further 3 to 5 days to show it.

## Exchanges
We don't process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order; if you are worried about stock, ask support to reserve the new item for 7 days.

## Faulty items
If an item is faulty, it is covered by our warranty rather than the returns policy, and there is no restocking fee. See the Warranty page for details.
