# Payments and Orders

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Prices on the website include UK VAT.

## Pay in instalments
On orders over £250 you can choose Klarna at checkout to pay in 3 interest-free instalments. Approval is decided by Klarna, not by Bean & Barrel.

## Changing or cancelling an order
You can change the delivery address or cancel an order until it is dispatched. Contact support as quickly as possible with your order number. Orders placed before 14:00 are often dispatched the same day, so changes may not always be possible. Once dispatched, the order must be returned under the returns policy instead.

## Discount codes
Only one discount code can be used per order, and codes can't be applied to orders that have already been placed. Discount codes cannot be used on gift cards or on items already marked as on sale.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are available from £20 to £500, are delivered by email, and are valid for 24 months. They cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Invoices
A VAT invoice is attached to your dispatch email. Business customers who need a company name on the invoice can request a new one from support within 90 days of purchase.
