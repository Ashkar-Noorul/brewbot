# Aurora Dual Boiler Espresso Machine

## Overview
The Aurora is our flagship home espresso machine, priced at £1,249. It has two stainless steel boilers, so you can pull a shot and steam milk at the same time. It weighs 22 kg and is 38 cm wide, 44 cm deep and 39 cm tall. It needs a standard UK 13 A socket (EU models ship with a Schuko plug).

## Key features
- PID temperature control, adjustable from 88 °C to 96 °C in 0.5 °C steps
- 2.5-litre removable water tank with a built-in water softening filter
- Pre-infusion adjustable from 0 to 10 seconds
- Shot timer on the display
- Commercial-size 58 mm portafilter; single and double baskets included
- Eco mode switches the steam boiler off after 30 minutes without use

## Getting started
1. Rinse the water tank and fit the softening filter cartridge.
2. Fill the tank and switch on. The machine fills the boilers automatically; this takes about 3 minutes on first use.
3. Wait for the "Ready" light, which takes about 12 minutes from cold.
4. Run two empty shots through the group head and purge the steam wand before your first drink.

## Water filter
Replace the softening filter every 2 months, or every 60 litres, whichever comes first. Replacement filters cost £12.50 each or £34 for a pack of three.
