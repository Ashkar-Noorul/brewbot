# Care and Cleaning Guide

## Daily
- Purge and wipe the steam wand after every use.
- Flush the group head for 2 seconds after each shot.
- Empty and rinse the drip tray.

## Weekly
Backflush with a blind basket and one Bean & Barrel cleaning tablet: run 10 seconds on, 10 seconds off, five times, then repeat with clean water. Soak the portafilter baskets in hot water with half a tablet.

## Descaling
Descale every 3 months, or when the machine shows E04. With the softening filter fitted and replaced on time, you may only need to descale every 6 months.
1. Remove the water filter cartridge.
2. Dissolve one sachet of Bean & Barrel descaler in 1 litre of water and pour into the tank.
3. Hold the "Menu" and "Hot water" buttons together for 3 seconds to start the descale cycle. It takes about 40 minutes.
4. Rinse the tank, fill with clean water and let the machine run its automatic rinse.
5. Refit the filter.

Only use descalers designed for espresso machines. Vinegar can damage the seals and is not covered by the warranty.

## Gaskets
Replace the group head gasket and shower screen roughly every 12 months. A replacement kit costs £18.
