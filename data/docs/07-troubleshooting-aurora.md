# Aurora Troubleshooting Guide

## Error E01: water tank empty
Refill the water tank and make sure it is pushed fully into place. If E01 stays on with a full tank, lift the tank and check that the float inside moves freely.

## Error E02: boiler fill timeout
The machine could not fill the boilers in time. This usually happens when the tank was removed during filling. Switch the machine off, refill the tank, wait 10 seconds and switch back on. If E02 returns, contact support.

## Error E03: temperature sensor fault
E03 means the brew boiler temperature sensor is giving an unexpected reading. Switch the machine off at the wall for 30 minutes so it can cool fully, then switch it on again. If E03 comes back, stop using the machine and contact support; this is covered by the warranty and is not something to repair at home.

## Error E04: descale required
The machine counts water use and shows E04 when it is time to descale. Follow the descaling steps in the Care and Cleaning Guide. E04 clears automatically once a descaling cycle has finished.

## Shot runs too fast (under 20 seconds)
Grind finer, use a slightly higher dose (18 to 19 g in the double basket), and check that you are tamping evenly. Old coffee (roasted more than 6 weeks ago) also runs fast.

## Shot runs too slow or chokes
Grind coarser, reduce the dose, and check the shower screen is not blocked with old coffee.

## Steam wand is weak or spits water
Make sure the steam boiler is on (eco mode may have switched it off) and wait for the steam light. Purge the wand for 2 seconds before steaming to clear condensed water. If the tip is blocked, soak it in hot water and clear the holes with the pin supplied.
